# Product: Sistema de Incidencias

A incident management system (sistema de incidencias) for tracking, reporting, and resolving incidents or issues within an organization.

## Core Purpose
- Allow users to report and track incidents
- Assign incidents to responsible parties
- Monitor incident status and resolution progress
- Generate reports on incident history and trends

## Key Concepts
- **Incidencia (Incident):** A reported issue, failure, or event that needs attention and resolution
- **Estado (Status):** The current state of an incident (e.g., abierta, en progreso, resuelta, cerrada)
- **Prioridad (Priority):** Severity level assigned to an incident
- **Asignado (Assignee):** The person or team responsible for resolving the incident
